🥙 Pani Puri

The king of Indian street snacks!

Ingredients:

  • 12–15 puris (hollow shells)
  • ½ cup boiled potatoes (mashed)
  • ¼ cup boiled chickpeas
  • 1 medium onion (chopped)
  • ½ tsp chaat masala
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
  • Tamarind chutney (to taste)
  • Spicy mint water (pani)

Steps:

  1. Crack the top of each puri gently.
  2. Fill with a spoon of mashed potato, chickpeas & onion.
  3. Sprinkle chaat masala & cumin powder.
  4. Add tamarind chutney for tangy sweetness.
  5. Dunk in chilled spicy mint water… and enjoy! 😋
