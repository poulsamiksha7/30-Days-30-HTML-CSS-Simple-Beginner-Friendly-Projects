Ayurvedic DIY • Skin Ritual

Calm, grounded skincare — a card‑based routine you can personalize by dosha

Color palette uses analogous earth tones with split‑complement accents (blue for Vata, gold for Pitta, green for Kapha) for a soothing but lively UI.

Vata Pitta Kapha Fragrance‑free • pH‑gentle • Minimalist
Vata
🫧

Cleanse (AM/PM)

Mix: 1 tbsp chickpea flour + splash of milk or oat milk + 1 tsp honey. Optional: a pinch of turmeric.

  • Massage onto damp skin 30–45s, rinse with lukewarm water.
  • Soothing, lightly moisturizing; keep the paste thin.
Pitta
🌿

Cleanse (Cooling)

Mix: 1 tbsp ground oats + cucumber juice or aloe + a few drops rose water.

  • Apply gently, avoid vigorous scrubbing.
  • Rinse cool; calms heat and redness sensations.
Kapha
🍃

Cleanse (Invigorating)

Mix: 1 tbsp chickpea flour + warm water + pinch of neem or tulsi powder.

  • Massage 45–60s, focusing on oilier zones.
  • Rinse warm; supports a fresh, matte feel.

Note: Patch‑test new ingredients, avoid if you’re allergic/sensitive, and consult a dermatologist for persistent skin issues.
